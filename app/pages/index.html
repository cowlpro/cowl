FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is COWL a mixer?

No. There is no shared pool and nothing is mixed. Each payment goes straight from the sender to a one-time address that belongs to the receiver. COWL never holds funds.

Can COWL take or freeze my money?

No. The router has no owner and no admin functions, and it never holds a balance. Only your spending key can move funds out of your one-time addresses. Note that stock token issuers can still pause their own tokens.

What if cowl.pro goes offline?

Your funds stay where they are. Announcements live on Robinhood Chain, and your keys come back from one signature, so any ERC-5564 tool can find and move them.

Which wallets work?

Any regular EVM wallet: MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom (enable Robinhood Chain under Active Networks) and others. Smart-contract wallets may sign differently each time, so they are not recommended.

Who pays gas to withdraw a token?

The sender. Each token payment carries a tiny amount of ETH (0.00002) to the one-time address, so you can withdraw without funding it first.

What does $COWL do?

$COWL is the community token for the project. The app is free and does not require it. Creator fees fund servers and, next, a relayer so withdrawals need no ETH at all.
