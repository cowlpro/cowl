Transparency

What the hood hides. And what it doesn't.

COWL uses stealth addresses (ERC-5564). It breaks the on-chain link between a payment and the person who receives it. It does not make transactions invisible. Here is the full picture.

On-chain visibility

InformationStatusWhy
Receiver's main walletHiddenFunds go to a one-time address derived from the receiver's public keys and a random value only the sender knows.
Link between two paymentsHiddenEach payment uses a new one-time address.
Receiver's private keysNever sharedDerived from a wallet signature inside the browser. Never sent to any server.
Amount and assetPublicTransfers on Robinhood Chain are public. COWL does not encrypt amounts.
Sender's walletPublicThe payer signs a normal transaction from their own wallet.
Withdrawal destinationPublicWithdrawing to your main wallet links that payment to it. Withdraw to a fresh wallet to stay hidden.
Short link (0x…)Names your walletThe short link is your wallet address. Whoever you share it with knows the payment is for that wallet. The chain still does not link the payment to it. Share the long link to avoid this.
Registered receive keysPublicRegistering a short link publishes your public keys next to your address. Incoming payments are still unlinkable.

Usage numbers on cowl.pro

ERC-5564 payments · all apps counts every stealth payment announced on Robinhood Chain by any app, because the Announcer is a shared standard contract. COWL is new; most of the second number is not COWL.

Contracts

ERC-5564 Announcer
Standard contract, deployed with the canonical CREATE2 address. Logs each payment.
ERC-6538 Registry
Standard contract. Maps a wallet to its public receive keys.
COWL RouterDeploying
Sends ETH or a token to the one-time address and calls the Announcer in the same transaction. No owner, no fee, no admin functions, never holds a balance. Source verified on Blockscout.

Open source

The full code behind cowl.pro is public: the router, the cryptography, the app and the indexer. The router compiled from that repository is byte-for-byte identical to the one deployed on Robinhood Chain; the README shows how to check it in one command. github.com/cowlpro/cowl

Not done yet

  • Independent audit: not yet. The router is short and verified; the cryptography follows ERC-5564 scheme 1 with the audited @noble/curves library.
  • COWL Names (in development): short names like cowl.pro/maya that point to your receive keys instead of your wallet. Claiming will require holding $COWL. Names will be public. No date until it is live and verified.
  • Relayer: withdrawals currently use a small amount of ETH sent along by the payer. A relayer is next.
  • Hidden amounts: not planned without zero-knowledge proofs and an audit. We will not claim it.

Outside COWL's control

Robinhood stock tokens are issued with controls: the issuer can pause transfers or block addresses. COWL cannot override this. Timing and amount patterns can also hint at who received a payment; vary them if that matters to you.

COWL is open-source software, not a bank or broker. Unaudited; use at your own risk.
